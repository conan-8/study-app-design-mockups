01 — Direction

Sprout

The Duolingo instinct, refined: fat radius, candy colors, and buttons you can physically press. Learning should feel like a toy you can't put down.

  • Baloo 2
  • Radius 20–26px
  • 3D press buttons
  • Spring easing
  • Personality: a coach that celebrates every rep. Zero intimidation.
  • Signature move: 5px bottom-edge buttons that sink when pressed — tactile reward.
  • Watch-out: can read as childish for university+ audiences; dial the saturation down for older users.

02 — Direction

Athenaeum

The Claude instinct: everything serif, nothing rounded, hairline rules instead of boxes. The app reads like a well-set book — calm, literate, quietly premium.

  • Newsreader
  • Radius 0
  • Hairline rules
  • Small caps
  • Personality: a librarian with excellent taste. Serious without being cold.
  • Signature move: no cards at all — rhythm comes from rules, italics, and numbering.
  • Watch-out: low visual energy; gamification must live in the copy, not the chrome.

03 — Direction

Pixel Terminal

The Kimi instinct, pushed further: strict black-on-black, bitmap type, scanlines, and keycaps instead of buttons. Studying as a homebrew console cartridge.

  • VT323 + Space Mono
  • Clipped corners
  • CRT scanlines
  • Cursor flicker
  • Personality: a machine that respects you. No cute mascot — just clean signal.
  • Signature move: keyboard-first UI with keycap hints; hover triggers terminal flicker.
  • Watch-out: monochrome means hierarchy must come from type scale and spacing alone.

04 — Direction

Card Catalog

Pure ink and paper, zero accent colors. Every screen is a ruled index card from a library drawer — stamped, dated, and due back Friday. Quietly obsessive.

  • IBM Plex Mono
  • Ruled lines
  • Stamps & dates
  • No accent color
  • Personality: archival, procedural, satisfyingly bureaucratic.
  • Signature move: progress and streaks rendered as due-date stamps and call numbers.
  • Watch-out: accessibility — monochrome UIs need generous contrast and spacing discipline.

05 — Direction

Acid Arcade

Console-achievement energy: acid lime and electric blue on black, trophies with locked "???" states, everything uppercase and grotesk. Study as ranked season.

  • Archivo Black
  • All caps, −0.03em
  • Trophy states
  • Opposing marquees
  • Personality: competitive, loud, built for streak culture and leaderboards.
  • Signature move: locked achievements as "???" placeholders — curiosity as the hook.
  • Watch-out: high stimulation; offer a focus mode that drops to greys only.

06 — Direction

Field Notes

The app as an explorable knowledge map: warm off-white, dashed borders, mono micro-labels, and one signature orange. Subjects are nodes; learning is traversal.

  • Sora + Space Mono
  • 7px radius cards
  • Dashed dividers
  • Visited = strike-through
  • Personality: a researcher's field notebook — spatial, curious, self-directed.
  • Signature move: finished cards get struck through; the current node blinks orange.
  • Watch-out: graph UIs need a simple linear fallback for small screens and tired brains.

07 — Direction

Herbarium

A pressed-flower journal for studying: botanical serif, sage greens, specimen numbering, italic annotations. Slow, deliberate, a little romantic.

  • Fraunces
  • № specimen numbers
  • Thin line engraving
  • 4px radius only
  • Personality: the app as a garden you tend. Growth metaphors everywhere.
  • Signature move: every deck is a "plate", every card a numbered specimen.
  • Watch-out: serif-heavy UIs demand large sizes; keep body text 17px+ on mobile.

08 — Direction

Examination

Swiss rigor with a disciplinary streak: red blocks, black rules, square corners, numbered everything. The app treats your session like a formal sitting — and you rise to it.

  • Archivo 800
  • 2px rules, 0 radius
  • Grid numbering
  • Red = action only
  • Personality: the strict examiner who secretly wants you to ace it.
  • Signature move: one red, used with total discipline — CTAs, streaks, warnings.
  • Watch-out: intensity fatigue; this language works best for exam-prep products specifically.

09 — Direction

Blueprint

Engineering-drawing aesthetics: cyan ink on navy vellum, dimension marks, mono type, crosshair registration. Every study session is a build with a spec sheet.

  • IBM Plex Mono
  • 26px grid
  • Dashed datum lines
  • + registration marks
  • Personality: precise, methodical, maker-culture. Knowledge is constructed.
  • Signature move: progress bars as dimension lines; decks labeled FIG. 1, FIG. 2…
  • Watch-out: dark mono UIs get samey fast — vary stroke weight and label density.

10 — Direction

Sorbet

Weightless and soft: frosted glass, gradient accents, pills with 100px radius, shadows that float instead of ground. The calmest possible way to open a study session.

  • DM Sans
  • Radius 22–99px
  • Backdrop blur
  • Gradient actions
  • Personality: gentle, airy, zero friction. Great for anxious learners.
  • Signature move: color lives only in gradients and blurs; surfaces stay white glass.
  • Watch-out: low contrast risk — always test text over the blurred gradients.
