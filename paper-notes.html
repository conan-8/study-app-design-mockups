Looseleaf

A design language that looks like your best class notes: warm paper with visible grain, ruled lines, taped corners, and a dog-eared flashcard. Serif headlines (Fraunces) carry the personality, a square sans (Sora) does the work, and mono labels handle the bookkeeping. Corners stay squared at 3px — but hard offset shadows, tilted sheets, and press-down buttons keep it playful. Right answers are green, wrong ones are red, unmistakable at a glance.
